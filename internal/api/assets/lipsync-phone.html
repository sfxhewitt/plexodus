Fix lip sync

Film the TV for ten seconds and Plexodus works out the delay and fixes the TV for you.

Type the code shown on the TV.

No code? On the TV open Settings, Lip sync, Fix with my phone.

  1. Sit where you normally watch, with the TV showing the flashing test.
  2. Tap Record the TV and film the whole screen for about 10 seconds (five beeps). Hold still.
  3. Tap Use Video. The TV fixes itself.
