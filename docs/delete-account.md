# Deleting your Plexodus account

Plexodus accounts live on the media server you connect to (yours, or the one
a friend invited you to). You can delete yours at any time from the app.

**On a TV (Android TV, Fire TV, Shield)**

1. Open Plexodus and go to **Settings** (your avatar, top right).
2. Choose **Delete my account**, then press it again to confirm.

**On a phone, tablet or in a browser**

1. Open Plexodus and tap your avatar (top right).
2. Tap **Delete my account**, then tap it again to confirm.

**What is deleted:** the account, its profiles, watch progress, watch history,
watchlist and sign-ins, straight away and permanently. Nothing is kept.

**Can't sign in?** Ask the person who runs the server to remove you (Settings >
Users > delete), or open an issue at
https://github.com/sfxhewitt/plexodus/issues and we will put you in touch.

The server owner's own admin account is removed by reinstalling or resetting
their server.
